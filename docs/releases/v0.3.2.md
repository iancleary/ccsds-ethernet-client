# v0.3.2

- Remove temporary checksum buffers from IPv4 and IPv6 UDP generation and
  validation. Public APIs, checksum policies, and wire behavior are unchanged.
- Verify generated checksums against an independent oracle at every supported
  payload length. Cover corruption, padding, and computed-zero encoding.
- Exercise all nine IPv4 checksum-policy combinations over Linux packet sockets.
- Document physical-peer checksum qualification and the evidence required before
  filtering, statistics sampling, receive batching, or packet mmap changes.

Five alternating trials per version across three complete virtual-link workloads
measured 15,000 to 9,000 process-wide allocation calls for 1,000 commands with
ACKs and telemetry. CPU and latency ranges overlapped. Queue-pressure trials
lost packets with both versions. These results establish allocation savings;
they do not establish a throughput improvement or physical-peer compatibility.

See `docs/performance-checksum.md` for workloads and results, and
`docs/checksum-qualification.md` for the remaining physical-peer procedure.
